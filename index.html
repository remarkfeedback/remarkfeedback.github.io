Remark

A comment bank that lives next to the essay. Click a comment and it goes into the student's work, highlighted and addressed to them by name.

  • SPaG, WWW / EBI and subject comment banks, ready to use
  • Marking codes with a key that builds itself as you mark
  • Summary boxes and DIRT tasks at the end of each piece

Free to use. Pro £19 a year. Whole school £99 a year.

For students and researchers

Themely

Qualitative coding without leaving Google Docs. Highlight a quote, click a code, and build up your themes as you go.

  • Group codes into themes and add a note to any quote
  • Send every interview to one Google Sheet, with counts for each code
  • Get a findings report with every theme and supporting quote

Free for 3 interviews. Dissertation Pass £9 for 6 months, or £19 a year.
