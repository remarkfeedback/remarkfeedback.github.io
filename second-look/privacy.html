Second Look privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026. Second Look is made by Survive Labs.

The short version

Second Look runs entirely on your device. We don't collect, store, sell or send anything you check with it. There's no account and no tracking inside the tool.

What happens when you use it

  • Messages and links you paste: checked by code running in your browser. They're never sent to us or anyone else. Second Look doesn't open the links you check.
  • Photos: read and redrawn in your browser to remove hidden details. The photo never leaves your device.
  • Checking a web page (Chrome extension): only when you click the Second Look button, it reads that page's address, title and visible text, and whether it has password or card boxes, to look for warning signs. This happens on your device and nothing is kept afterwards.
  • Right-click checks (Chrome extension): the text or link you choose is passed to Second Look's own page on your device, held only until it's checked, then cleared.

Permissions the Chrome extension uses

  • activeTab and scripting: to check the page you're on, only when you click the button.
  • contextMenus: to add "Take a second look" to the right-click menu.
  • storage: to pass a right-clicked text or link to Second Look's own page. It's cleared straight away.

The extension doesn't have permission to read your other tabs or your browsing history, and doesn't run in the background on the sites you visit.

Our website

The Second Look page on survivelabs.co.uk uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies. It doesn't see anything you paste or choose in the tool.

Its limits

Second Look looks for common warning signs. It can't prove something is safe, and it can miss scams. If you're unsure, contact the company using a number or website you already know.
