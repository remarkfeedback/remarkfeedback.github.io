Privacy policy

Seller Sync (Chrome extension) · Last updated 16 November 2026

In short: Seller Sync collects no personal data. The sales files you import are read inside your own browser, and your sales are stored only on your computer. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Who we are

Seller Sync is made by Survive Labs, based in the United Kingdom. Contact: hello@survivelabs.co.uk.

What Seller Sync does with your data

  • Files you import (for example sales reports you download from Etsy, eBay or Depop) are read by the extension inside your browser. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Your sales records (dates, item names, amounts, fees and anything you type in) are saved using Chrome's local extension storage on your computer only.
  • Backups and exports are files that you choose to download to your own computer.

What Seller Sync does not do

  • It has no servers and no user accounts.
  • It does not read, change or access any website, including Vinted, eBay, Depop or Etsy. It never asks for your selling-account login.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data.

Permissions

  • storage / unlimitedStorage: to save your sales on your computer, however many you have.

Seller Sync requests no access to websites, tabs or browsing history.

Deleting your data

Use Export → Delete all sales inside Seller Sync, or uninstall the extension, which removes everything it stored.

Links

Seller Sync contains links to other Survive Labs products on Payhip and to platform help pages. If you follow them, those websites' own privacy policies apply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
