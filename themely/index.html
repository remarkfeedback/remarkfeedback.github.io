Thematic analysis, right in Google Docs

Highlight a quote, click a code. Themely helps you code interview transcripts, group codes into themes and turn them into findings you can write up. No new software to learn.

Coming soon to the Google Workspace Marketplace.

What it does

  • Tag quotes with codes. Select words, click a code. Quotes can carry several codes, and highlights stay put as you edit.
  • Group codes into themes, so your codebook grows into your analysis.
  • Send every interview to one results sheet, with counts for each code and a code-by-interview table.
  • Create a findings report: a Google Doc with every theme, code and supporting quote, plus a codebook table for your appendix.
  • Turn existing highlights into codes, if you've already started with the highlighter.
  • Anonymise names in one go, and share your codebook with a supervisor or research partner.

Help

I've paid. How do I unlock Pro?
Open Themely in any Google Doc and click "Already paid? Unlock". Use the same Google account you opened the buy link from. If it still doesn't unlock after a minute, email us with the email address you paid with.

Where is my data kept?
In your own Google account: your documents, a results sheet and reports in your Drive. We never receive your transcripts. See the privacy policy.

A highlight disappeared after I moved some text.
Cutting and pasting coded text can remove its code. Tag it again, or use Undo straight after tagging if you picked the wrong code.

Anything else?
Email survivelabs@outlook.com. We'll get back to you as soon as we can.
