Privacy policy

Themely: qualitative coding for Google Docs and Google Sheets · Last updated 18 November 2026

In short: Themely works inside your own Google account. Your interviews, quotes and notes stay in your Google Docs, Sheets and Drive. We never receive them, and Themely has no servers or database of its own. The only thing that leaves Google is your email address, sent to our payment provider to check whether you have bought Pro.

Who we are

Themely is made by Survive Labs, based in the United Kingdom. Contact: hello@survivelabs.co.uk.

What Themely can access, and why

When you install Themely, Google asks you to allow these permissions:

  • The document you open it in (documents.currentonly). Themely highlights and tags the quotes you choose, and can replace names when you use "Anonymise names". It cannot open or read any other document.
  • Showing its sidebar (script.container.ui), so Themely can display its panel next to your document.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets). Themely creates your results sheet and adds your tagged quotes and counts to it. Google describes this permission broadly, but Themely only ever opens the results sheet it created for you. It never opens your other spreadsheets.
  • Files Themely creates in your Drive (drive.file), used to save your findings report as a Google Doc. This permission only covers files Themely made. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • Your Google account email address (userinfo.email), used only to check whether you have bought Themely Pro.
  • Connecting to an external service (script.external_request), used only to ask our payment provider, Stripe, whether a Themely payment exists for you.

What Themely stores, and where

  • In your document: the highlights you add, plus invisible markers that remember which words carry which code. These are part of your document, like any other formatting.
  • With your document (Google's Apps Script storage for that document): your notes (memos) on quotes, the list of names and pseudonyms you enter under "Anonymise names", and the last quote you tagged so Undo works. Anyone who can edit the document and opens Themely in it can see these notes and the names list. To keep real names private, remove them from the list (✕) once you have anonymised the document. This deletes them from the saved list.
  • In your Google account (Apps Script storage linked to your account): your codebook (code names, colours, meanings and themes), links to your results sheet and latest report, which interviews you have coded (used for the free plan's limit), and whether your Pro plan is active.
  • In your Google Drive: your results sheet and findings reports. You own these files, and they stay in your Drive if you stop using Themely.
  • In your browser: a single note that you have seen the welcome guide.

Themely has no servers or database of its own. We never receive, read, sell or share your documents, transcripts, quotes or notes.

What leaves Google

When Themely checks your plan, it sends your email address to Stripe and asks whether a Themely payment matches it. The buy buttons in Themely also attach a scrambled account code made from your email, so a payment still matches if you pay with a different email address. The code cannot be turned back into your email. Nothing from your documents is ever sent.

Themely for Sheets

Themely for Sheets is a separate add-on for Google Sheets, used to tag written answers in a survey or any column of text. It follows the same rules: it works inside your own Google account, it has no servers or database of its own, and we never receive your survey answers.

When you install it, Google asks you to allow these permissions:

  • The spreadsheet you open it in (spreadsheets.currentonly). Themely reads the answer column you choose, writes the codes you apply into a "Themely:" column, shades coded answers, and builds the "Themely summary" and "Themely by group" sheets in that same spreadsheet. It cannot open any other spreadsheet.
  • Showing its sidebar (script.container.ui), so Themely can display its panel next to your spreadsheet.
  • Your Google account email address (userinfo.email), used only to check whether you have bought Themely Pro.
  • Connecting to an external service (script.external_request), used only to ask Stripe whether a Themely payment exists for you.

What it stores, and where:

  • In your spreadsheet: the codes you apply (as text in the "Themely:" column), the cell shading, and any summary sheets you build.
  • With your spreadsheet (Google's Apps Script storage for that file): your codebook and themes for that survey, and which column you are coding. Anyone who can edit the spreadsheet and opens Themely in it can see these.
  • In your Google account: whether your Pro plan is active.

When Themely for Sheets checks your plan, it sends only your email address (and the scrambled account code described below) to Stripe. Nothing from your spreadsheet is ever sent. One Themely Pro purchase covers both Docs and Sheets.

Research participants

Many people use Themely on interview transcripts that contain other people's personal data. That data stays in your own Google account (or your institution's), and Survive Labs never receives it. You remain responsible for following your ethics approval, consent terms and your institution's data protection rules, including where your Google account stores data.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe. We see your name and email address, the plan you bought, and the payment amount and date. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us. See Stripe's privacy policy. We keep payment records as UK tax law requires.

Use of Google user data

Themely's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide Themely's features as described on this page. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never transferred to anyone except Stripe for the plan check described above.

AI and machine learning. Themely does not use AI or machine-learning models. Google user data, including your transcripts, survey answers, quotes and notes, is never used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models, and is never sent to any third-party AI service.

How we protect your data

Themely has no servers or database of its own, so your documents, spreadsheets and notes never leave Google's systems and are protected by Google's own security. The plan check is sent to Stripe over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and contains only your email address and the scrambled account code. Payment records are held by Stripe, and we view them only through Stripe's secure dashboard.

How long we keep data

Everything Themely saves (highlights, notes, codebooks, results sheets and reports) stays in your own documents, spreadsheets and Google account until you delete it. We never hold a copy. Payment records are kept by Stripe for as long as UK tax law requires (normally six years), then deleted.

Children

Themely is made for university students and researchers. It is not intended for children under 13.

Deleting your data

  • Your results sheets and reports are ordinary files in your Drive. Delete them like any other file.
  • To remove highlights from a document, delete the codes in Themely, or delete the document.
  • To stop all access, uninstall Themely (Google Docs → Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons), or remove it from your Google account's third-party access page.
  • To ask us to delete your payment records (where the law allows), or for help clearing stored settings, email hello@survivelabs.co.uk.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email us and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
