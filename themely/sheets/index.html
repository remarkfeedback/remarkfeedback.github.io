How it works

  1. Pick your questionOpen the spreadsheet your Google Form fills, choose the column of written answers, and click Code this column.
  2. Make a few codesAdd short labels for the ideas you expect, such as homework load. Add more as new ideas appear.
  3. Tag each answerThemely shows one answer at a time. Click a code, or press 1 to 9, and it jumps to the next answer you haven't coded.
  4. Build your summaryOne click adds a summary sheet with how often each code came up, example quotes and a chart.

Questions

What does Themely for Sheets do?

It helps you sort written survey answers into themes. You give each answer a short label (a code), such as “homework load” or “lunch queue”, and Themely counts how often each one comes up, with example quotes and a chart.

Does it work with Google Forms?

Yes. Open the spreadsheet your Google Form sends its responses to, pick the question column, and start coding. It works on any column of text in Google Sheets.

What can Themely see in my Google account?

Only the spreadsheet you open it in. It can't see your other files, and your answers stay in your own Google account.

Is there a free version?

Yes. The free version gives you up to 10 codes and 50 coded answers per spreadsheet. Pro removes the limits and adds group comparisons.

Do I need to pay again if I already have Themely for Docs?

No. One Themely Pro or Dissertation Pass purchase unlocks Themely in both Google Docs and Google Sheets.
