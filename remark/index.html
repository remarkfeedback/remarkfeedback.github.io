How it works

  1. Pick a commentSPaG, WWW / EBI and subject banks sit right next to the essay. Search, or click.
  2. It lands in the essayHighlighted, in the right place, addressed to the student by name.
  3. Drop in marking codesSp, P, C and more. A key explaining every code builds itself at the end.
  4. Finish with a summaryWhat Went Well, Even Better If, a target and a DIRT task, in one tidy box.

How does Shakespeare present ambition in Macbeth?

Jordan Smith, Year 10

Shakespeare presents ambition as a dangerous force. When Macbeth hears the witches, he immediately thinks about murder.

Lady macbeth[C] calls on spirits to unsex her[P] This shows she wants power so much she will give up her feminitity[Sp]

In conclusion, ambition destroys both characters.

Add Remark to Google Docs

Remark is free on the Google Workspace Marketplace. The free version includes the SPaG and WWW / EBI banks, 15 marking codes, summary boxes, DIRT tasks and up to 15 of your own comments. Pro adds the GCSE subject banks, the re-teach report and unlimited comments for £19 for 12 months, with no auto-renewal.

Once it's installed, open any Google Doc and choose Extensions → Remark → Open Remark.
