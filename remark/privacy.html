Privacy policy

Remark: comment bank for Google Docs · Last updated 18 November 2026

In short: Remark works only inside the Google Doc you open it in. It never reads your other files, never sends student work anywhere, and keeps your settings in your own Google account.

Who we are

Remark is made by Survive Labs, based in the United Kingdom. Contact: hello@survivelabs.co.uk.

What Remark can access

  • The current document only (Google permission documents.currentonly). Remark inserts the comments, marking codes and summary boxes you choose into the document you have open. It cannot open, read or change any other document or Drive file.
  • Showing its sidebar (script.container.ui), so Remark can display its panel next to your document.
  • Your Google account email address (userinfo.email), used only to check whether you have bought Remark Pro.
  • Connecting to an external service (script.external_request), used only to ask our payment provider, Stripe, whether a Pro payment exists for your email address or your school's email domain.

What Remark stores, and where

  • Your settings, custom comments and usage counts are stored in Google's Apps Script storage linked to your own Google account. Usage counts record which comments you used. They contain no student work and no student names.
  • The student first name for a document is stored with that document only, so comments can be personalised.
  • Remark has no servers or database of its own. We do not collect, sell or share your documents or any student data.

What leaves Google

The only information Remark sends outside Google is your email address, sent to Stripe to check for a Pro licence. Stripe returns whether a matching payment exists. Nothing from your documents is ever sent.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe. We see your email address, the product bought and, for school licences, the school email domain entered at checkout. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Use of Google user data

Remark's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide Remark's features as described here. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never transferred to others except Stripe for the licence check described above.

AI and machine learning. Remark does not use AI or machine-learning models. Google user data, including the content of your documents, is never used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models, and is never sent to any third-party AI service.

How we protect your data

Remark has no servers or database of its own, so your documents and settings never leave Google's systems and are protected by Google's own security. The licence check is sent to Stripe over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and Remark is only allowed to contact Stripe's API (api.stripe.com). Payment records are held by Stripe, and we view them only through Stripe's secure dashboard.

How long we keep data

Your settings, custom comments and usage counts stay in your own Google account until you clear them or ask us to help remove them. We never hold a copy. Comments Remark adds to a document are part of that document and stay until you delete them. Payment records are kept by Stripe for as long as UK tax law requires (normally six years), then deleted.

Children's data

Remark is a tool for teachers. Any student information in a document stays in that document, under your school's control.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Remark (Google Docs → Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons) to stop all access. To have your stored settings removed, email hello@survivelabs.co.uk and we will help you clear them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email us and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
