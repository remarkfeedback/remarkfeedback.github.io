Common questions

Where do I find Remark? In Google Docs: Extensions → Remark → Open Remark.

I bought Pro but it hasn't unlocked. Open the Pro tab and click “I've paid: unlock Pro”. Make sure you paid with the same email address as the Google account you use for Docs. Payments can take a minute to appear.

The sidebar is blank or shows an error. If you're signed in to more than one Google account in your browser, Google add-ons only work in the account you signed in to first. Open the document in a browser window where only your school account is signed in.

How do I get a refund? Email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.

How do I remove Remark? Google Docs → Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons → Remark → Uninstall.
