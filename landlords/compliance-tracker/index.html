    Where do these dates come from?

    Gas: a Gas Safe registered engineer must check every gas appliance and flue you provide every 12 months. Give existing tenants a copy within 28 days, and new tenants before they move in. You can have the check done from 10 months after the last one and keep the same deadline. HSE guidance

    Electrical: the installation must be inspected and tested by a qualified person at least every 5 years, or sooner if the report says so. Give tenants a copy of the report.

    EPC: certificates last 10 years. Today you can't let a property rated F or G unless it has a registered exemption. The government plans to raise the minimum to C from 1 October 2030, with legislation still to follow.

    Deposits: protect the deposit in a government-approved scheme within 30 days of receiving it.

    Landlord database: registration opens region by region from 15 December 2026, and every landlord in England must be registered by 14 November 2027. Your deadline follows each rental property's location, not where you live: West Midlands properties open on 15 December 2026 with a deadline of 14 March 2027. Find each property's deadline.

    Is this legal advice?

    No. It's a reminder tool built from official guidance. Always go by the dates on your actual certificates and reports, and get advice if your situation is unusual.
