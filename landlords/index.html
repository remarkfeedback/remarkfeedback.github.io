The rules at a glance

England. The basics every private landlord has to get right.

12monthsGas safety checkBy a Gas Safe registered engineer. Copy to tenants within 28 days.
5yearsElectrical report (EICR)At least every 5 years, or sooner if the report says so.
Eor aboveEPC ratingCertificates last 10 years. C is planned from October 2030.
30daysProtect the depositIn a government-approved scheme, and send the tenant the details.
Day 1Alarms workingSmoke and carbon monoxide alarms checked on the first day of each tenancy.
BeforesigningWritten statementEvery new tenant gets the written statement of terms first.
2monthsTenant's noticeTenants can usually leave with two months' notice at any time.
