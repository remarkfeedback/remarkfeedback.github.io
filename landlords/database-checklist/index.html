        How much does it cost?

        £65 per property per year, with no VAT. Registering yourself as a landlord is free, and so are updates. During the rollout your first fee is reduced to cover the part-year, and all your properties then share one renewal date. Source: GOV.UK guidance.

        What if something is missing on the day?

        You can still register and add missing information within 28 days, or the property's registration may be deactivated. After that, you have 28 days to update your record whenever something changes, such as a new gas certificate.

        What happens if I don't register?

        Councils can fine landlords up to £7,000 for not registering themselves or a property, and up to £40,000 for false or misleading information or repeated breaches. Once a region opens, tenants can also apply for a rent repayment order.

        Is this legal advice?

        No. It's a free checklist built from GOV.UK guidance as of November 2026. Check GOV.UK before you register, as details can change.
