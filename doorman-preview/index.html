Questions

Does Doorman use AI?

Doorman doesn't use AI to judge anything. Every warning comes from our own rules, and every check runs on your device. That's why it can always tell you exactly what it noticed.

If Doorman finds nothing, does that mean a site is safe?

No, and we'll never say it is. New scams appear every day, and a brand-new scam site can look perfectly clean. Doorman looks for the warning signs scammers usually leave. If something still feels wrong, contact the company using details you already trust.

Can Doorman see my passwords or card numbers?

Doorman notices when you click into a password or card box on a risky page, so it can warn you. It never reads, stores or sends what you type.

Why does Chrome say it can "read and change data on all websites"?

That's Chrome's standard wording for any extension that checks pages for you. Doorman only asks for it when you switch on Always-on or Marketplace Guard, and the checks still happen on your computer.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome first, which also covers Microsoft Edge and Brave. We'd like to bring it to phones later.

Who makes Doorman?

Doorman is made in the UK by Survive Labs. We build small, honest tools that help people stay safe and private online.
