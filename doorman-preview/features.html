Scan this page Free

Click the Doorman icon, then Scan this page. Doorman marks anything suspicious with numbered markers and explains each one in a side panel.

What it looks for

  • A page that uses a company's name but isn't on that company's website
  • Forms that send your card or password to a different site
  • Password or card boxes on pages that aren't secure
  • Pressure: countdowns, threats, "final notice"
  • Links that say one address but go to another

Example: a fake parcel page

Strong signs of a scam
  1. Uses the name "Royal Mail" but isn't their website
  2. This form sends what you type to collect-pay.xyz
  3. It asks for a small fee

Check a message Free

Paste a text, WhatsApp message or email. Doorman reads it for the tricks used in the UK's most common scams.

What it looks for

  • Fake deliveries, fines, tax refunds and bank alerts
  • "Hi Mum, new number" and family emergencies
  • Requests for codes, passwords, card details or ID photos
  • Gift card, crypto and "safe account" payments
  • Words disguised to slip past filters, like "acc0unt" or hidden characters

Example

Hi Mum, I've dropped my phone down the toilet, this is my new number. Can you help with a bill today? x
Likely scam
  1. It claims to be family on a new number
Call them on the number you already have.

Check a link Free

Paste a link to see where it really goes, without opening it.

What it looks for

  • Misspellings of real companies (paypa1, amazom)
  • Letters from other alphabets that look identical to English ones
  • A real company's name used at the start of someone else's address
  • Cheap web endings popular with scam sites, and random-looking names
  • Link shorteners that hide the destination

Example

https://www.nhs.uk.appointment-check.top/login
Likely scam
  1. The address starts like the NHS's, but goes somewhere else
This link really goes to appointment-check.top

Check a QR code Free

Drop in a photo of a QR code, from a parking meter, letter or poster, and see where it leads before you scan it with your phone.

What it looks for

  • Codes that lead to payment pages that aren't the official app
  • Crypto wallet and payment requests
  • Open Wi-Fi networks that anyone can watch

Example: a sticker on a parking meter

Some warning signsThis code goes to quick-park-pay.site
  1. The address ends in .site
  2. The website name has several hyphens
Pay through the council's or parking company's own app instead.

Check an attachment Free

Drop in a file before you open it. Doorman looks inside without running it.

What it looks for

  • Programs pretending to be invoices or PDFs (Invoice.pdf.exe)
  • Office files with macros
  • Programs hidden inside zip files
  • File names that use hidden characters to reverse their ending

Example

parcel.zip
Don't open this
  1. The zip contains a program: Parcel_label.exe

Check a password Free

An honest idea of how quickly a password could be guessed, based on the patterns people really use. It's checked on your computer and never stored.

What it looks for

  • A word plus a year and a symbol, the most common pattern
  • Keyboard runs, repeats and names
  • Swapped letters like "P@ssw0rd" that don't fool anyone

Example

Liverpool2024!
Very weak: could be guessed in seconds
  1. It contains a common word
  2. It contains what looks like a year
  3. It follows a very common pattern
A long phrase of random words is far stronger.

Marketplace Guard Pro

Reads chats on selling and rental sites as messages arrive, and speaks up the moment someone tries a known trick.

What it looks for

  • Links to "receive your payment" or "confirm your sale"
  • Bank transfers, PayPal friends and family, or paying someone else
  • Moving the chat to WhatsApp or off the site
  • Overpaying and asking for the difference back
  • Deposits before a viewing, and landlords who are "abroad"

Works on Vinted, eBay, Gumtree, Facebook Marketplace, Depop, SpareRoom, Rightmove, Zoopla, OpenRent and AutoTrader.

Example

Sent the money. Click here to receive your payment: secure-pay-confirm.top/receive
Sends a link to "receive" your paymentYou never need a link to receive money.

Known scam websites list Pro

Every day Doorman downloads a list of hundreds of thousands of reported phishing websites, and checks every site you visit against it on your computer.

Why it's built this way

  • The whole list lives on your computer, so looking a site up tells nobody where you've been
  • The download only fetches the list. Nothing about you is sent
  • Popular genuine sites are left off, so you don't get false alarms
  • New scam sites appear every hour, so Doorman's own checks still look at every page too
