Who this is for

Directors' home addresses are kept private these days, but older filings and some company addresses are still public, and most people don't know they're there.

  • You set up a limited company from home and used your house as the registered office
  • You gave your home as your correspondence (service) address
  • You own rental property through a limited company
  • You've resigned or the company has closed, but the old filings are still public

How it works

The fiddly part is finding every document. The form only has room for a few, each one has to be listed exactly, and Companies House rejects mistakes. That's the part we do.

  1. Tell us who you are

    Pay £29 and give us your name and one of your companies. Takes two minutes.

  2. We go through every filing

    We check each company you're linked to, back to 2003, and list every document that shows your home address and can be removed.

  3. You get a finished form

    Within 3 working days we email your completed SR01 form, the list of documents and the exact fee to pay.

  4. You send it off

    Pay Companies House online and email them the form. They review each document and confirm when your address is gone.

Questions

Can I remove my current registered office address?

Not while it's still in use. You change your registered office to another address first, for example a registered office service, and then the old one can be removed. We'll tell you exactly what to do.

What if you don't find anything that can be removed?

We refund your £29 in full.

Does this remove my address completely?

It removes it from the public documents anyone can download. Companies House still keeps it privately, as the law requires. If a UK address was a registered office, they may leave the first part of the postcode showing.

Do you send the form for me?

You send it yourself, so the Companies House fee goes straight from you to them and the application comes from you. It's one email.

Are you lawyers or accountants?

No. We're a small UK privacy service. We do the searching and paperwork; Companies House makes the decision on each document. If you're at risk of violence or intimidation, Companies House has a separate process, and you should contact the police if you're in danger.
