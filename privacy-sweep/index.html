Questions

Is this legal?

Yes. UK data protection law gives you the right to ask companies to delete your personal data, and lets someone act for you with your written permission. That's what we do.

What can't be removed?

Some records are public by law, such as property sales. Your report lists exactly what can and can't be removed, before we send any removal requests.

Can't I just do this myself?

You can, and many of the fixes are free. The hard part is knowing where to look, doing it in the right order so your details don't come straight back, and chasing the sites that ignore you. That's what you're paying us for. If you'd rather do it yourself, Report only gives you every step.

How long does it take?

Your report arrives within 5 working days of your form and permission letter coming back. Companies have up to a month to act on a removal request, and we chase any that don't.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel within 14 days of booking and we'll refund you in full if we haven't started your search. If we have started at your request, we'll refund the part we haven't done yet. Email hello@survivelabs.co.uk.

Who is it for?

Anyone, but especially online sellers, company directors, landlords and anyone who'd rather a stranger couldn't find their front door.
