  • Who those sites say you live with

What happens next

  1. We reply to tell you whether a place is still free and how we confirm that you are the person being searched for.
  2. If a place is yours, we send a short form and a permission letter. We only search for you, and only once you've given written permission.
  3. A person checks your report before we send it to you.
  4. Afterwards, we ask for honest private feedback. A public quote is entirely optional, and if you agree to one we'll say you received the report free.
Please don't send your home address in a Facebook message. We'll ask for what we need by email once your place is confirmed. Once the 3 places are gone, we'll say so here. You can still get a report for £19.
