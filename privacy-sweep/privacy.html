How Privacy Sweep handles your data

Last updated 17 November 2026

Privacy Sweep is a service from Survive Labs, based in the United Kingdom. It isn't open to customers yet. Right now you can join a waitlist by email.

While you're on the waitlist

If you email us to join, we keep your email address, your name if you give it, and anything you write in your message. We use it only to tell you about Privacy Sweep, including when your place is ready. We don't sell it, share it or use it for anything else.

Our legal basis is your consent. You can ask us to remove you from the waitlist and delete your details at any time by emailing survivelabs@outlook.com.

When the service opens

Before anyone becomes a customer, we'll publish a full privacy policy explaining exactly what we collect to find and remove your details, how long we keep it, who processes it for us and how we protect it. Our commitments won't change:

  • We only ever search for the person who's paying, after an ID check.
  • We collect only what's needed to find and remove your details.
  • We never sell or share your details, and we delete your ID check once it's done.
  • We act for you only with your written permission.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Email survivelabs@outlook.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
