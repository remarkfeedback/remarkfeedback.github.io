How Privasweep handles your data

Last updated 25 November 2026

Privasweep is a service from Survive Labs (Regan Sweetman), a UK business. Our postal address is Lytchett House, 13 Freeland Park, Wareham Road, Poole, Dorset BH16 6FA. We find where your personal details are published online and help get them removed. To do that we have to handle some of the very details you want hidden, so this page explains exactly what we collect, why, who helps us and how long we keep it.

Questions or requests: hello@survivelabs.co.uk.

Who we search for

We only ever search for the person who books and pays. Before we start, we check that the name and email on your form match your payment, and you sign a letter giving us permission to search and act for you. We won't search for anyone else unless that person signs their own letter.

Free exposure check: we only check the person asking, using the name and town they give us, after they confirm the details are their own. We don't run free checks on anyone else.

What we collect

  • If you ask for a free check: your name, your town or postcode, any other names or previous town you give us, your email address and your confirmation that the details are your own. We record how many places we found your details and what kind of details they show, but not links or screenshots.
  • If you use our contact form: your name, email address and message.
  • When you book: your name, email address and payment details. Stripe takes your card details; we see only your name, email, the amount and the last four digits of your card.
  • Your sign-up form: your name and any other names you've used, your current and previous home addresses, the email addresses and phone numbers you want checked, your year of birth if you choose to give it, any companies you're a director of, and anything you tell us you're worried about.
  • Your permission letter: the permission wording you agree to, your typed signature, and the date and time you gave it.
  • What we find: links to and screenshots of the pages where your details appear.
  • Removal requests: the requests we send for you and the replies we get.
  • If you leave a review: your rating, review, the name you choose to show (first name and surname initial), and your review code, which we use only to check you are a paying customer. We never publish where you live. We publish the review and name only with your permission, and remove it if you ask.

We never ask you to email copies of your passport or driving licence. If a website insists on ID before removing your details, we'll tell you and you send it to them directly.

How we use it, and why we're allowed to

  • To search for your details, write your report and send removal requests on your behalf. Our legal basis is the contract you make with us when you book.
  • To keep records of payments, as tax law requires. Our legal basis is legal obligation.
  • To run a free check you ask for and email you the result. The result email includes the option to book a report. Our legal basis is your request. We won't email you again unless you book or write to us.
  • To reply to messages sent through our contact form. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you.

We don't use your details for marketing, we don't sell them, and we don't use them for anything other than your sweep.

Who we share it with

  • The websites and organisations we ask to remove your details. We send them only what they need to find and remove your record, plus a copy of your permission letter.
  • Stripe, which takes your payment.
  • Microsoft (Outlook), which runs our email.
  • Google, which stores the form you fill in after paying, your permission record, free check requests and contact form messages in our private Google account, and sends our confirmation and result emails.
  • Anthropic, whose Claude tools hold our customer tracker and working documents and help us organise findings and draft removal requests.

Visiting our website: we count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites or identify you; we only see totals, such as how many people viewed a page.

Google Ads: when we run Google ads, our free check page tells Google Ads that a check was requested, so we can see whether an ad led to it. It runs in Google's consent mode with advertising cookies turned off, and sends no name, email or other details you type into the form.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

How we protect it

Your details are kept only in our private customer tracker (a private Google Sheet and folder), your job's working documents and our email inbox, all behind password-protected accounts. Only Survive Labs can see them. Nothing from your sweep is ever published, shared or used in marketing, even with your name removed.

How long we keep it

  • Screenshots and working notes: deleted 30 days after your final report.
  • Your form, permission letter, report and request records: kept for 12 months after your service ends, in case a site puts your details back or you have a question, then deleted. Monitoring customers' records are kept while monitoring continues.
  • Payment records: kept for as long as tax law requires, usually six years.
  • Free check requests and results: deleted automatically 90 days after you ask.
  • Contact form messages: deleted automatically after 24 months.

You can ask us to delete your details sooner, except where we must keep payment records by law.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it. You can also withdraw your permission letter at any time, and we'll stop acting for you. Email hello@survivelabs.co.uk.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

If you emailed us before we opened

If you joined our waitlist by email, we keep your email address and message only to tell you about Privasweep. Ask us any time and we'll delete them.
