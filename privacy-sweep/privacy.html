How Privacy Sweep handles your data

Last updated 18 November 2026

Privacy Sweep is a service from Survive Labs (Regan Sweetman), based in the United Kingdom. We find where your personal details are published online and help get them removed. To do that we have to handle some of the very details you want hidden, so this page explains exactly what we collect, why, who helps us and how long we keep it.

Questions or requests: survivelabs@outlook.com.

Who we search for

We only ever search for the person who books and pays. Before we start, we check that the name and email on your form match your payment, and you sign a letter giving us permission to search and act for you. We won't search for anyone else unless that person signs their own letter.

What we collect

  • When you book: your name, email address and payment details. Stripe takes your card details; we see only your name, email, the amount and the last four digits of your card.
  • Your sign-up form: your name and any other names you've used, your current and previous home addresses, the email addresses and phone numbers you want checked, your year of birth if you choose to give it, any companies you're a director of, and anything you tell us you're worried about.
  • Your permission letter, signed by you.
  • What we find: links to and screenshots of the pages where your details appear.
  • Removal requests: the requests we send for you and the replies we get.

We never ask you to email copies of your passport or driving licence. If a website insists on ID before removing your details, we'll tell you and you send it to them directly.

How we use it, and why we're allowed to

  • To search for your details, write your report and send removal requests on your behalf. Our legal basis is the contract you make with us when you book.
  • To keep records of payments, as tax law requires. Our legal basis is legal obligation.

We don't use your details for marketing, we don't sell them, and we don't use them for anything other than your sweep.

Who we share it with

  • The websites and organisations we ask to remove your details. We send them only what they need to find and remove your record, plus a copy of your permission letter.
  • Stripe, which takes your payment.
  • Microsoft (Outlook), which runs our email.
  • Anthropic, whose Claude tools hold our customer tracker and working documents and help us organise findings and draft removal requests.

Visiting our website: we count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites or identify you; we only see totals, such as how many people viewed a page.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

How we protect it

Your details are kept only in our private customer tracker, your job's working documents and our email inbox, all behind password-protected accounts. Only Survive Labs can see them. Nothing from your sweep is ever published, shared or used in marketing, even with your name removed.

How long we keep it

  • Screenshots and working notes: deleted 30 days after your final report.
  • Your form, permission letter, report and request records: kept for 12 months after your service ends, in case a site puts your details back or you have a question, then deleted. Monitoring customers' records are kept while monitoring continues.
  • Payment records: kept for as long as tax law requires, usually six years.

You can ask us to delete your details sooner, except where we must keep payment records by law.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it. You can also withdraw your permission letter at any time, and we'll stop acting for you. Email survivelabs@outlook.com.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

If you emailed us before we opened

If you joined our waitlist by email, we keep your email address and message only to tell you about Privacy Sweep. Ask us any time and we'll delete them.
