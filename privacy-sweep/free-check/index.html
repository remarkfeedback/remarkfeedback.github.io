Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no card and no catch. You get a real result either way. You only pay if you choose to see the full details or want us to remove them.

Why don't you show me where straight away?

Finding and checking each listing properly takes time, and the full report also covers more places than the free check. The free result tells you whether it's worth going further.

Can I check someone else?

No. The free check is only for your own details. That protects people from being looked up by someone else.

What happens to my details?

We use them only to run your check and send your result, and delete them after 90 days. We never sell or share them. Read our privacy policy.

How long does it take?

Within 24 hours, usually the same day.
