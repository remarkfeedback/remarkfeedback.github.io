Questions

Is this legal?

Yes. UK data protection law gives you the right to ask companies to delete your personal data, and lets someone act for you with your written permission. That's what we do.

Will I lose my vote?

No. You'd come off the open version of the electoral register, which is the one sold to marketers and people-search sites. You stay on the full register and can still vote.

What can't be removed?

Some records are public by law, like Land Registry sales. We'll tell you exactly what can and can't be removed before you pay for the done-for-you service.

How do you protect my details?

We only collect what's needed to find and remove your information, we never sell or share it, and we delete your ID check once it's done.

Who is this for?

Anyone, but especially online sellers, company directors, landlords and anyone who'd rather a stranger couldn't find their front door.
