What it checks

The tricks behind most UK scams, explained so you can spot them yourself next time.

  • MessagesPressure to act now, requests for codes, card details or a "safe account", gift cards and crypto, "Hi Mum" texts, fake delivery fees and refunds.
  • LinksWhere a link really goes, copies of well-known names like Royal Mail, HMRC or PayPal, look-alike letters, hidden destinations and short links. We never open the link.
  • PhotosPhone photos often hide the exact spot they were taken. Selling on Vinted or advertising a room? We show what's hidden and give you a clean copy.
