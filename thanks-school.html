Your school licence is active

Every teacher whose Google account uses your school email domain can now unlock Remark Pro. Share this page with your colleagues.

  1. Install Remark

    In Google Docs open Extensions → Add-ons → Get add-ons, search for Remark and click Install.

  2. Open Remark

    In any document choose Extensions → Remark → Open Remark, signed in with your school Google account.

  3. Click Unlock Pro

    Go to the Pro tab and click Unlock Pro. Remark recognises your school's licence straight away.

Need help?

The licence covers accounts ending in the email domain entered at checkout, for example @yourschool.org.uk. If a colleague can't unlock, check they're signed in with their school account, then email survivelabs@outlook.com with your receipt.

A receipt is on its way to your email. Licences last 12 months and don't renew automatically.
